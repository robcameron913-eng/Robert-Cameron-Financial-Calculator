Formula Explanations

Holding Period Return: (Ending Price - Beginning Price) / Beginning Price

Annualized Return: Converts the selected period return into a yearly return.

Volatility: Standard deviation of daily returns, annualized using √252 trading days.

Beta: Measures SAIA's systematic risk compared to the market.

Correlation: Measures how closely SAIA and SPY move together.

Alpha: Measures performance above or below what CAPM predicts.

Sharpe Ratio: Measures risk-adjusted return.
