Formula Explanations

Holding Period Return: (Ending Price - Beginning Price) / Beginning Price

Annualized Return: Converts total return into a yearly return.

Volatility: Standard deviation of daily returns, annualized.

Beta: Measures SAIA's risk compared to the market.

Alpha: Measures performance above or below what CAPM predicts.

Sharpe Ratio: Risk-adjusted return using volatility.
